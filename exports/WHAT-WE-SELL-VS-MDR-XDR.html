What we sell vs MDR / XDR

One-page positioning · Kestrel / Kevantic MSSP Control Plane · Customer demo & CISO Q&A

One-line position: We deliver Managed Detection & Response (MDR) with a co-managed customer portal, plus optional MXDR add-ons. AI assists; analysts decide; customers see only approved summaries. We are not a fully autonomous AI SOC and not a managed firewall vendor.
