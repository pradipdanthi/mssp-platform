Add-on

Threat Intelligence & Enrichment

Context on alerts — so analysts chase real risk.

What this service offers

This service enriches security events with reputation and adversary context so true high-risk activity rises above noise.

It is an enrichment layer on top of detection.

  • Indicator matching against curated threat feeds
  • 90-Day Retrospective Threat Hunting (Instant Zero-Day Retro-Sweeps) via Junexis Retrospective Engine
  • Attack-technique mapping on alerts
  • Reputation scoring for IPs/domains in context
  • Earlier warning when campaigns match your environment profile

Limitations (honest scope)

Clear boundaries help you buy the right module — not oversell.

  • Intelligence is context — not a standalone detection stack.
  • Feed coverage is never 100% of the internet; novel attacks may not have prior indicators.
  • Does not replace Core monitoring or NDR.
  • Customer portal shows safe enrichment summaries, not raw intel dumps.

Who should go for it

  • Teams with alert fatigue who need better prioritization.
  • SOC-backed customers who want adversary context in cases.

Usually not the best fit if…

  • Buyers seeking only a threat-intel newsletter with no platform integration.
