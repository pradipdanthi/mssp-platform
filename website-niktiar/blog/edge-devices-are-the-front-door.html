Edge devices are the new front door

The box with the firewall logo is often the most public computer you own.

28 October 2026 · 3 min read · Kevantic Cyber Security

Companies still picture the edge as a locked gate at the front of the building. In practice the firewall management page, the VPN, and the remote desktop gateway are computers on the internet. If they answer from anywhere, they are a front door. In Microsoft’s 2025 incident investigations, 12 percent of breaches used exposed remote services, and 18 percent started from an unpatched website. Those appliances are scanned constantly. A known flaw, or a password that still works, is enough to get in.

Why a shared pipe is a weak answer

A useful edge is dedicated to one customer. Your traffic, your rules, and your admin logins should not share a pipe with a neighbour you have never met. When something looks wrong, you want the record of that device, not a blended feed you cannot separate.

What NikTiar SecureEdge is for

NikTiar SecureEdge is that dedicated edge: web application controls, DNS filtering, a next-generation firewall, and ransomware exposure controls. Detection and response still come from the 24/7 Cloud SOC. One hundred percent of the raw logs stay on the NikTiar Edge Node. You do not ship every log to a third-party cloud in order to be watched.

Pair the edge with a list of what is already published. An edge rule cannot close a name you did not know you owned.

A check you can do without a project

List every way a person reaches the company from outside a laptop on the desk: VPN, supplier portal, remote support tool, firewall admin page, cloud console. For each one, write who receives an alert when the login is wrong several times, and who receives an alert when the login is right but unusual. If the answer is “the device keeps a local log we open after something breaks,” that door is unattended.

Then ask whether that device is dedicated to you. A shared security service that mixes your admin events with other customers’ events will struggle to tell you, quickly, that your gateway was the one being tried. NikTiar SecureEdge is dedicated per customer for that reason. The Cloud SOC still does the watching. The raw record of the edge stays on your NikTiar Edge Node, so a later investigation is not trapped in someone else’s platform.

Edge work also fails when the company has names on the internet that never pass through the edge you know about. That is a separate list, and it is why external attack surface belongs in the same month as a firewall review.

Ask whoever runs the edge today: is the device’s own admin page reachable from the internet, and who is told when a login to it fails repeatedly? If nobody is told, the front door has no bell.
